=========================================================================
 MODEL LIMITATIONS AND REQUIRED VALIDATION
=========================================================================
  HIGHER-FIDELITY CFD REQUIRED FOR:
    - the surface-piercing model in its entirety: water entry and exit, spray
      sheet, added mass, cavity closure and the ventilated section polars. The
      Level-2 cyclic loads reconstruct immersion KINEMATICS only and therefore
      UNDERESTIMATE peak loads.
    - rotor-rotor interaction at 50 mm spacing, which is strongly unsteady.
      Sliding-mesh (or overset) transient CFD is needed; the steady actuator
      superposition used here cannot capture blade-passing effects.
    - the section Cl/Cd polars, which are currently a provisional analytical
      model, not data. This is the single largest source of uncertainty in the
      absolute efficiency numbers.
    - the minimum pressure coefficient used for cavitation inception.
    - free-surface interaction of the propulsor with the hulls.
  STRUCTURAL FEA REQUIRED FOR:
    - blade root stress, fillet stress concentration, and skew-induced moments.
    - blade natural frequencies against blade-rate and shaft-rate excitation,
      which are NOT computed here at all and which matter for a surface-piercing
      propeller that is impulsively loaded once per revolution.
    - shaft, bearing and hub design under the cyclic loads reported above.
  GEARBOX MECHANICAL DESIGN REQUIRED FOR:
    - everything beyond the ratings specified here: architecture, bearings,
      thrust path, lubrication, seals, and the coaxial shaft arrangement.
  CAVITATION TUNNEL TESTING REQUIRED FOR:
    - cavitation inception and extent on the submerged variant.
  TOWING TANK / FREE-SURFACE TESTING REQUIRED FOR:
    - the resistance curve itself beyond the five supplied points, the 300 kg
      case, wake fraction and thrust deduction.
    - ventilation inception and the thrust-breakdown boundary.
  FULL-SCALE WATER TESTING REQUIRED FOR:
    - the motor efficiency map, gearbox efficiency, and the achieved Wh/nm.
  
  WHAT THIS TOOL IS: a transparent, parameterised preliminary design and
  trade-study framework whose every assumption is declared and replaceable.
  WHAT IT IS NOT: a validated performance prediction. Blade element momentum
  theory with empirical corrections is not equivalent to CFD, and the
  surface-piercing corrections in particular are the weakest part of the chain.
