# Rules changes, Monaco Energy Class 2026 → 2027

Source documents, both in `docs/reference/`:

- `Technical_Rules_Energy_Class_2027_V1.pdf`, version 2027.1, issued 07/09/2026
- `MEBC_Notice_of_Challenge_2027_V1.pdf`, version 1.0, issued 04/09/2026

The 2026 documents are kept in `archive-2026/` rather than deleted, because
design decisions already recorded in `notes/` were made against them.

Method: every `ENERGY_REQ_n vX.Y` token was extracted from both documents and
compared by identifier and version, then each difference was read in full.
Both years contain 174 requirements. **Four are new, four were withdrawn, and
eleven were revised.**

---

## 1. The change that matters most

### ENERGY_REQ_188 v1.0 → v1.1 — the 25 kW power cap tightened

| | Text |
|---|---|
| **2026** | The total **nominal** power consumption of the motor(s) shall not exceed 25 kW. |
| **2027** | The sum of **instantaneous** power consumption of all motors shall not exceed 25 kW. *Note: No power exceeding 25 kW will be allowed, even during a peak.* |

This is a real tightening, not a rewording. "Nominal" left room to argue that a
short peak above 25 kW was permitted. "Instantaneous", with an explicit note
about peaks, closes that. It also now applies to the **sum over all motors**,
which matters for any multi-motor architecture.

**Effect on this project: none, and that is the point.** `propulsor/config.m`
already enforces `P_cap_W = 25e3` for every duration. What changes is the
justification: this is a competition rule, not a team preference, so it is not
negotiable and the optimiser must never be allowed to breach it.

## 2. New requirements

### ENERGY_REQ_194 v1.0 — motor seat structural load

> The motor seat structure shall withstand a torque load equivalent to at least
> 200% of the maximum torque generated by the motor at full power.
> *Note: The design should integrate peak power to full power.*

**Directly affects the propulsor and the frame.** With a 100 N·m maximum motor
torque, the motor seat must be designed to **200 N·m**. This is a new structural
case for `structures/` and for `propulsor/structural_model.m` when it is
written. Nothing in the repository currently checks it.

### ENERGY_REQ_195 v1.0 — power sensor mounting volume

> The team shall provide a free mounting volume of 220 × 111 × 80 mm (L × W × H)
> for each power sensor device provided by the Organiser [...] This area shall
> be water proof [...] one sensor per traction chain.

**Affects the cockpit layout.** A waterproof 220 × 111 × 80 mm volume per
traction chain. A single motor means one volume. `cad/scripts/layout.py` and
`pack_fit.py` do not currently reserve it.

### ENERGY_REQ_193 v1.0 — battery chemistry, from August 2028

> From August 2028, lithium-ion batteries shall use LFP chemistry. Solid-state
> batteries are also permitted. Any other battery technology shall require prior
> approval from the Technical Committee.

**Not binding for the 2027 event, but decisive for the pack after it.** The
Molicel INR21700-P50B is an NMC cell; "INR" denotes nickel-manganese-cobalt.
The entire `powertrain/` model is built around it. It is compliant for 2027 and
would not be compliant from August 2028 without Technical Committee approval.

Worth raising now rather than after the pack is built.

### ENERGY_REQ_192 v1.0 — hull protection

> When off the water, the hulls shall be adequately protected.

Operational. No design effect.

## 3. Withdrawn requirements

All four were in the 2026 rules and are absent from 2027.

| Requirement | 2026 text | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ENERGY_REQ_153 | Transponder for tracking may be provided by the Organisation and shall be positioned on each boat | Withdrawn; not referenced in this repository |
| ENERGY_REQ_174 | Bulkhead hole diameter shall be no less than 30 mm | **Withdrawn; checked by our compliance code** |
| ENERGY_REQ_175 | Bulkhead hole shall not exceed 50 mm and shall be covered with a removable cap | **Withdrawn; checked by our compliance code** |
| ENERGY_REQ_176 | The hole shall not be oriented directly to the pilot, but on his side | **Withdrawn; checked by our compliance code** |

The bulkhead hole specification was removed entirely, with no replacement.
`ENERGY_REQ_55`, requiring grommets on wire holes through the bulkhead,
survives unchanged.

`powertrain/08_compliance/P50B_MonacoCompliance.m` checks 174 and 175 and
mentions 176. Those checks are now marked **WITHDRAWN 2027** rather than
deleted, so the audit trail of what was once required survives.

## 4. Revised requirements

### Affecting design

**ENERGY_REQ_186 v1.0 → v1.1 — hydrofoils banned**

| | Text |
|---|---|
| 2026 | No cockpit appendices shall be in contact with the water, except for the motor and the water pump. |
| 2027 | **Hydrofoils are not permitted.** No cockpit appendices shall be in contact with the water, except for the motor and the water pump. |

The prohibition is now explicit. Note this does **not** affect
`propulsor/hydrofoil_polar.m`, which concerns the aerofoil sections of the
propeller blade, not lifting foils on the hull. The naming collision is
unfortunate; the file is correctly named for what it does.

**ENERGY_REQ_184 v2.0 → v1.1 — monitoring power interface**

Maximum power raised from **30 W to 35 W**, and the connector part numbers
changed from `BD-02BFFA-LL7001` / `ABD-02AFFM-LL7A03` to `AD-02BFFA-LL7001`,
`1AD-02BFFM-LL7AXX` or `AAD-02BFFM-LL7AXX`, all Amphenol LTW. **Check what has
been ordered.**

**ENERGY_REQ_185 v2.0 → v1.1 — external monitoring volume**

Volume unchanged at 190 × 150 × 122 mm and still open to the sky, but 2027 adds
that it must be **at least 40 cm above the beams of the hulls** and outside all
closed compartments. That is a new placement constraint on the cockpit.

**ENERGY_REQ_93 v1.0 → v1.1 — surface temperature**

"Exposed parts shall not reach more than 60 °C" became "**Any reachable
surface** shall not exceed a temperature of 60 °C". Broader: a surface a hand
can reach now counts even if it is not exposed. Relevant to
`powertrain/07_simulation/P50B_ThermalDesign.m`.

### Clarifications with no numerical effect

| Requirement | Change |
|---|---|
| ENERGY_REQ_7 v1.2 → v1.3 | 10 kWh cap and every energy factor unchanged. Typographical only: "define" → "defined". |
| ENERGY_REQ_48 v3.0 → v3.1 | **250 kg limit unchanged.** Notes renumbered; hull mass still 65 ±1 kg. |
| ENERGY_REQ_2 v1.1 → v1.2 | Hull build spec unchanged. New Note 2 permits Organiser-approved alterations that give no competitive advantage, with weight adjustment. |
| ENERGY_REQ_155 v1.1 → v1.2 | New note: nested battery containers each need an overpressure valve. |
| ENERGY_REQ_181 v1.0 → v1.2 | ESC input power diagram, now cross-referenced from the new ENERGY_REQ_195. |
| ENERGY_REQ_56 v2.0 → v2.1 | Fire extinguisher port. Text identical. |
